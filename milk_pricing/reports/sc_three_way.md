## Regional context

Across all 26 sampled HT stores, the Carolinas run far below the mid-Atlantic: NC/SC cluster at $2.89–$3.39 while Virginia, Maryland and Delaware stores sit at $3.89–$4.39. Harris Teeter's SC prices are not typical of its chain.

## Limits

- Walmart and HT are single sampled stores per market; treat gaps under ~$0.15 as noise. Aldi is zone-verified and can be trusted tighter.
- Only Greenville has all three retailers. Charleston has Aldi and HT but no sampled Walmart; five markets have Aldi alone.
- Rock Hill (29707) was reached but its page carried no parseable private-label whole gallon.
- Aldi is a delivered price; Walmart is confirmed no-markup; HT is its own site price. These are not all the same kind of number.
