## Reading

- **The Upstate is the price floor.** Inman ($2.17) and Greer ($2.36) are the two cheapest SC stores in the sample. Both sit in the Spartanburg-Greenville corridor. Aldi operates across SC, but the Upstate is where Aldi and Lidl overlap most densely, so the hard-discount floor Walmart prices against is lowest there.
- **Rural markets carry the premium.** Williston ($3.72) is the SC high and $1.55/gal above Inman — the same SKU, same state. Williston is a small CSRA town with no discounter nearby.
- **SC runs below the country.** SC median $2.74 against a national median of $3.14. South Carolina is a low-price milk state for Walmart.
- **The coastal markets converge.** Conway, Myrtle Beach and Belton all sit at exactly $2.74, as do two Charlotte stores — evidence of genuine zone pricing rather than store-by-store drift.

## Caveats

- Stores are sampled from random proxy exits, not chosen. Coverage is opportunistic: 7 SC stores over 5 trade areas, with Columbia, Charleston and Rock Hill not yet hit.
- One SKU (whole gallon). Fat tiers and pack sizes are covered in the ladder analysis, not here.
- Prices are Walmart-only. The competitor set needs Instacart, still gated on the zone's Premium domains permission.
- 58% of raw fetches were third-party marketplace substitutions and are excluded; see README. Only pages whose primary item is the benchmark SKU are counted.
