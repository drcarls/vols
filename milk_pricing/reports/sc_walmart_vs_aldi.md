## Caveats

- Walmart rows are single sampled stores, Aldi rows are zone-verified. A Walmart store can sit off its own zone's modal price, so treat gaps under ~$0.15 as noise.
- Matched markets are limited to the 6 trade areas where a Walmart store was sampled. Columbia, Charleston, Rock Hill, Sumter and Hilton Head have Aldi but no Walmart comparison yet.
- Aldi prices come from its own Instacart-powered site, which may differ from in-store shelf price; read them as the delivered price.
- Publix, Food Lion and Harris Teeter remain blocked behind Bright Data's Premium domains permission, so the conventional channel is absent from this comparison.
