Two rules that keep it honest and effective

What makes a score a wedge

It's computable before they're a customer

If the score needs their internal data, it's a project, not a wedge. The whole power is walking in with the number already on the table.

It's a confidence signal, not a verdict

Overclaim and a sharp buyer catches you. Lead with "here's what's observable, here's the gap." Certainty comes from the fix — because that's where their real data enters.

We give you the score that shows the gap. We're the engine that turns your data into the fix — an actionable competitive advantage.

Who owns which layer. Commodity signals — anyone, and that's fine (they're the fuel). The prediction engine — UpliftIQ. The proprietary outcome data — the client, and it's the moat. The context mapping — the domain crosswalk that translates signal into the vertical's language and actions — is the expertise-gated layer, curated once per vertical and reused. Agents commoditise the signal; they don't commoditise the mapping.
