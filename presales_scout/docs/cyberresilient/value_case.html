02 · What CyberResilient does today

A self-assessment platform, done well

The product, as it stands — an AI-powered cyber-resilience platform:

  • •NIS2 maturity assessment — a questionnaire with instant scoring and recommendations ("your free NIS2 score, 80% faster").
  • •Risk register — structured risks with treatment plans, owners, and status.
  • •Business continuity — critical-process mapping, RTO/RPO, recovery scenarios.
  • •Audit-ready reporting — board- and regulator-ready PDF export.
  • •Trend tracking — multi-assessment comparison over time.

The through-line: it begins, every time, with what the customer tells you — a self-report. That's the strength (fast, no integration) and the ceiling.

03 · The ceiling

What a self-report alone can't do

  • ✕It's self-graded. Customers rate themselves; you can't verify it. A board and a regulator both discount self-declared maturity.
  • ✕It's gated on engagement. Nothing happens until the prospect sits down and fills in the questionnaire.
  • ✕It's a snapshot. Posture drifts between assessments; the questionnaire only sees the day it's filled in.
  • ✕The benchmark is thin. Comparing self-reports to self-reports compares beliefs, not reality.
