Concept

Self-reported is only half the picture.

Your maturity assessment asks the customer what they know. But customers grade their own homework — and you can't fix what you don't know. An outside-in, evidence-based score fills the other half: what's observable about them, with no questionnaire. It works three ways at once — a proactive outbound list, a free front door that pulls prospects in, and an evidence layer that strengthens every module inside the platform.

All at once

Three motions, one engine

1 Outbound · proactive

Reach out to the weakest

Score NIS2-scope orgs in bulk; the bottom-quartile ones are a ready outbound list. Open with their own number: "your external posture ranks bottom of your peer group — see the full picture free." Fills the funnel without waiting.

2 Front door · inbound / PLG

A score before they sign up

Your free NIS2 score needs a questionnaire first. The observed score needs nothing — lead with it: "here's your external posture — claim your full assessment to go deeper." A zero-effort top of funnel that feeds sign-up.

3 Evidence layer · in-product

Validate the self-assessment

Inside the platform, put observed evidence next to the self-report: "you rated email 'good' — externally your DMARC is p=none." Turns a subjective questionnaire into an evidence-checked one.

And the part that compounds

The decision history is the real moat

The observed engine is just a sensor — anyone can build one. UpliftIQ is the decision-optimization platform on top: it records every recommendation and what happened next, and refines the next one. That decision-and-outcome history — across all your customers — can't be copied and compounds every cycle. Two things get sharper with every org you onboard:

Real peers, not just signals

Rank a customer against the actual maturity of similar orgs on your platform — a percentile only you can compute, because only you hold the corpus. The observed layer adds the objective cross-check.

✦ Suggestions

What actually worked

"Orgs like you closed this gap by doing X" — recommendations learned from cross-customer outcomes, not a static rulebook. Powers the "AI recommendations" you already promise, and gets better with scale.

The observed score says what's wrong; UpliftIQ's decision history says what fixes it — and gets better every cycle. The engine is leverage; the decision-and-outcome history is the defensibility.

Why it fits CyberResilient specifically

Data sovereignty, kept. The engine reads only public records — DNS, certificate logs, procurement, the target's own site — and never touches the customer's systems or needs their data. That sits cleanly inside your Sweden-only, GDPR, MSB/NCC-grade story: objective evidence with no new data-handling risk. And it's multi-framework already — NIS2, ISO 27001, CIS map today; NIST CSF 2.0 is a one-file addition to the crosswalk.

Self-report tells you what they know. Observed evidence tells you what's true. CyberResilient becomes the platform that shows both.
